How to use

  1. Pre-loaded: Black dots = sampled points from current perfdata.js (visual reference only). New dots, or black dots you drag, are the only points used for fitting. Blue = old original, Red = your edited/fitted.
  2. Pick Panel/Curve via dropdown or click a panel, then click to add points or drag existing points. Right-click removes.
  3. Or edit the table directly — type new m, e, b. Change is instant. Leave e empty for linear m*x+b, fill e for b*exp(e*x).
  4. Fit all from points overwrites table with least-squares fit from your points.
  5. Export copies snippet to paste into scripts/perfdata.js. Also saved to localStorage automatically.
  6. Python: python3 tools/afm_fitter.py --demo --model linear --export /tmp/new.js
